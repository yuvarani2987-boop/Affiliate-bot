Affiliate AI Bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Affiliate AI Bot helps creators prepare product media, captions, and videos and connect their own TikTok accounts for authorized publishing.

Information we process

When you choose to connect TikTok, the service may receive account identifiers, profile information permitted by the scopes you approve, and OAuth access tokens needed to provide the requested TikTok features. The service may also process product links, images, videos, prompts, captions, and other content you choose to provide.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide requested features such as authentication, media preparation, caption generation, and authorized publishing to the TikTok account selected by the user. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

Content may be sent to service providers selected for features you request, such as TikTok APIs and configured AI services. Information is not shared for unrelated advertising purposes.

Data retention and security

We retain data only as needed to operate the service and use reasonable measures to protect it. Users should not share passwords or secret API credentials with the application.

User choices

You may disconnect your TikTok account and stop using the service at any time. TikTok permissions can also be managed from your TikTok account settings.

Children

This service is not intended for children under the minimum age required to use TikTok or applicable developer services.

Changes

We may update this policy when the service changes. The latest version will be posted at this URL.
